# Insurance AI Advantage System — Signature Skills

Signature Skills are fully engineered insurance capabilities designed for operational orchestration inside the Insurance AI Advantage System.

They go beyond individual prompts by combining:

- Insurance-specific reasoning
- Structured workflows
- Decision rules
- Evidence and source controls
- Execution readiness
- Human decision boundaries
- Workflow state
- Ownership and handoffs
- System-of-record controls
- Quality assurance
- Structured downstream outputs

## Signature Skill Architecture

Foundational Skills make individual insurance expertise repeatable.

Signature Skills combine multiple forms of insurance expertise into deeper operational capabilities.

AI Teammates apply those capabilities to real workflows.

AI Workforces coordinate multiple AI Teammates across the agency.

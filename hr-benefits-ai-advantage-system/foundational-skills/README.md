# HR Benefits AI Advantage System — Foundational Skills

## 25 Practical AI Skills for the Work HR & Benefits Teams Do Every Day

The **Foundational Skills** collection converts the original 25 prompts from **The HR Benefits AI Advantage System** into reusable, structured AI capabilities built around real HR and Employee Benefits workflows.

These Skills are designed to help HR and Benefits professionals reduce repetitive administrative work, improve accuracy, communicate more consistently, and create more capacity for strategic work.

> **A prompt tells AI what to do. A Skill provides a repeatable framework for how the work should be performed.**

The original prompts are the starting point.

The Skills add the operating structure required for repeatable execution.

---

# Foundational Skills vs. Signature Skills

The HR Benefits AI Advantage System contains two distinct Skill tiers.

## Foundational Skills

Foundational Skills focus on a specific recurring task or capability.

Examples:

- Extract deadlines from an SPD
- Reconcile HSA contributions
- Draft an Open Enrollment announcement
- Explain a complex benefit
- Build a leave timeline
- Generate an executive Benefits ROI report

They are designed to be:

- Reusable
- Focused
- Practical
- Easy to invoke
- Composable with other Skills
- Useful as standalone AI capabilities

---

## Signature Skills

Signature Skills combine multiple capabilities into larger, fully engineered workflows.

Examples include:

- Monthly Reconciliation Intelligence Engine
- Open Enrollment Command Center
- Intelligent Benefits Concierge
- New Hire & Termination Automation Engine
- FMLA & Leave Management Coordinator
- Employee Benefits Communications Suite

The progression is:

```text
Foundational Skill
        ↓
Signature Skill
        ↓
Operational System
        ↓
AI Teammate
        ↓
Agentic Workflow
```

# 01 — Plan Documents & Compliance

Skills for turning dense benefits documentation and recurring compliance responsibilities into clear, actionable workflows.

These Skills should help HR teams identify:

- Requirements
- Deadlines
- Owners
- Employee actions
- Required documentation
- Consequences when explicitly stated
- Items requiring professional review

## Skills

- [ ] **1.1 Extract Key Deadlines from SPD / Plan Documents**  
  Converts SPDs, plan documents, plan changes, and carrier information into a structured list of requirements, deadlines, responsible parties, employee actions, and material compliance considerations.

- [ ] **1.2 Annual Compliance Checklist Generator**  
  Builds a prioritized compliance checklist for Open Enrollment, ACA reporting, COBRA administration, dependent verification, audits, and other recurring benefits requirements.

- [ ] **1.3 Policy Interpretation & Summary**

# 03 — Reconciliation & Billing

Skills for identifying financial leakage, eligibility mismatches, contribution errors, and administrative discrepancies across disconnected benefits systems.

This category is one of the strongest starting points for the HR Benefits AI Advantage System because reconciliation work is:

- Recurring
- Data-driven
- Time-consuming
- Error-prone
- Financially measurable

## Skills

- [ ] **3.1 Monthly Carrier Bill Reconciliation**  
  Compares carrier invoices against internal eligibility information to identify billing discrepancies, quantify financial impact, prioritize corrections, and improve future reconciliation.

- [ ] **3.2 HSA Contribution Reconciliation**  
  Compares payroll HSA contribution data against trustee or administrator posting information to identify missing, incorrect, duplicate, or inappropriate contributions.

- [ ] **3.3 New Hire & Termination Reconciliation Engine**  
  Reviews new hires and terminations to identify carrier eligibility actions, effective-date issues, COBRA implications, billing impact, and required follow-up.

- [ ] **3.4 Dependent Eligibility & Status Change Reconciliation**  
  Reviews births, adoptions, marriages, divorces, aging-off dependents, and other status changes to determine required documentation and benefits actions.

- [ ] **3.5 Reconciliation Summary Report for Leadership**  
  Converts detailed reconciliation findings into an executive-level view of recovered dollars, recurring issues, process weaknesses, and improvement opportunities.

# HR Benefits Safety Standard

HR and Benefits Skills operate in a domain where employee privacy, plan governance, effective dates, eligibility, and compliance can materially affect people.

Every Skill should therefore apply the principle:

> **Anonymize → Generalize → Summarize → Review → Approve**

---

## Anonymize

Do not use employee identity unless the task genuinely requires it and the AI environment is approved for that information.

Prefer:

```text
Employee A
Employee B
Dependent A
```

where possible.

---

## Generalize

Use aggregated or generalized information when exact personal data is unnecessary.

---

## Summarize

Avoid uploading complete employee files when a structured summary is sufficient for the task.

---

## Review

AI output is not automatically authoritative.

Review against applicable:

- Plan documents
- SPDs
- Carrier documents
- Employer policies
- Eligibility rules
- HRIS data
- Payroll data
- Compliance guidance
- Applicable law

---

## Approve

Qualified humans retain responsibility for final decisions involving:

- Eligibility
- Coverage
- Enrollment
- COBRA
- Leave
- Claims
- Employee communication
- Compliance
- Legal interpretation
- Financial corrections

### Your people make the decisions. Your AI Teammates do the work.

# Recommended Adoption Approach

Do not attempt to deploy all 25 Skills at once.

Use the system philosophy:

> **Keep It Simple. Start Small. Scale Fast.**

A practical sequence is:

```text
1. Select one painful recurring workflow
        ↓
2. Use the Skill on real work
        ↓
3. Review the output
        ↓
4. Refine the process
        ↓
5. Repeat until consistent
        ↓
6. Measure the improvement
        ↓
7. Connect it to the next Skill
```

Reconciliation is a strong starting category because improvements can often be measured through:

- Time saved
- Billing discrepancies
- Overpayments identified
- Corrections completed
- Recurring errors prevented

# Plan Documents & Compliance

## HR Benefits AI Advantage System — Foundational Skills

This category contains Foundational Skills designed to help HR and Benefits teams turn complex plan documents, compliance requirements, carrier information, and internal policies into clear, actionable operating intelligence.

The objective is not to replace legal, regulatory, plan-administrator, or benefits-professional judgment.

The objective is to help HR teams more efficiently identify:

- Requirements
- Deadlines
- Responsible parties
- Employee actions
- Required documentation
- Areas requiring additional review
- Potential compliance or operational risk

# Category Operating Principles

Skills in this category should strongly apply the following HR Benefits Profile standards:

- Governing Document Standard
- Benefits Source Hierarchy
- Document Completeness Standard
- Regulatory Currency Standard
- Jurisdiction Standard
- Obligation Extraction Standard
- Effective-Date Integrity
- Human Review Escalation
- Assumption Visibility
- Auditability

These Skills should never treat general AI knowledge as a substitute for the applicable plan document, carrier document, employer policy, regulation, or authorized professional determination.

---

# Governing Source Principle

For plan-specific questions:

> **The governing document controls.**

AI may:

- Extract
- Summarize
- Organize
- Compare
- Translate
- Identify deadlines
- Identify obligations
- Flag ambiguity

AI should not:

- Invent requirements
- Invent deadlines
- Invent penalties
- Override plan language
- Provide unsupported legal conclusions
- Resolve material ambiguity without appropriate review

# Apeironix Skills

Open-source AI Skills for insurance professionals, agencies, and AI builders.

Apeironix Skills turn repeatable insurance expertise into reusable AI capabilities.

> **A prompt tells AI what to do. A Skill provides a repeatable framework for how the work should be performed.**

The library is organized into two distinct capability tiers:

**Foundational Skills → Signature Skills → AI Teammates → AI Workforce → AI Operating Layer**

# Insurance AI Advantage System

The **Insurance AI Advantage System** represents the next layer of the Apeironix Skills architecture.

Foundational Skills make individual insurance expertise repeatable.

**Signature Skills** are fully engineered insurance capabilities designed for operational orchestration. They combine multiple reasoning disciplines, workflow controls, evidence standards, decision rules, structured data, human decision boundaries, and downstream handoffs into higher-value capabilities designed for AI Teammates and AI Workforce execution.

[Explore the Insurance AI Advantage System](./insurance-ai-advantage-system/README.md)

## Signature Skills

1. [Commercial Insurance Underwriter Submission Narrative](./insurance-ai-advantage-system/signature-skills/01-underwriter-submission-narrative/SKILL.md) — Transforms validated commercial risk intelligence into a concise, underwriting-ready submission narrative designed to improve evaluation speed, credibility, and quote efficiency.

2. [Commercial Insurance Coverage & Risk Advisory Analysis](./insurance-ai-advantage-system/signature-skills/02-coverage-risk-advisory-analysis/SKILL.md) — Maps real-world business exposures against the current insurance program to identify material coverage gaps, limit concerns, restrictions, and prioritized advisory opportunities.

3. [Commercial Insurance Executive Proposal Narrative](./insurance-ai-advantage-system/signature-skills/03-executive-proposal-narrative/SKILL.md) — Converts technical insurance options into a clear executive recommendation with normalized comparisons, transparent trade-offs, decision logic, and an implementation-ready client path forward.

4. [VIN Replacement Value Intelligence](./insurance-ai-advantage-system/signature-skills/04-vin-replacement-value-estimator/SKILL.md) — Decodes VIN-supported vehicle identity, separates confirmed facts from inferred configuration, develops transparent replacement-value scenarios, scores confidence, and identifies the highest-value data needed to finalize valuation.

# From Skills to an AI Workforce

The Apeironix architecture is designed to compound capabilities rather than create isolated prompts.

### Foundational Skills

Turn individual insurance tasks and expertise into repeatable AI capabilities.

### Signature Skills

Combine multiple forms of reasoning, workflow logic, governance, structured outputs, and decision support into fully engineered operational capabilities.

### AI Teammates

Apply Skills to specific roles and workflows inside an insurance organization.

### AI Workforce

Coordinates multiple AI Teammates across connected workflows.

### AI Operating Layer

Connects AI work across the systems insurance organizations already use, including:

- CRM
- Agency Management Systems
- Carrier portals
- Documents
- Email
- Accounting
- Claims systems
- Benefits platforms
- Other insurance technology

**Keep your systems. Activate an AI Workforce.**

# AI Insurance Automation Guide — Skills Collection

### From Prompts to Reusable AI Skills for Insurance Professionals

The original Apeironix **AI Insurance Automation Guide** introduced 21 practical prompts designed to help insurance professionals use AI more effectively across common agency workflows.

This collection takes that concept one step further.

Each original prompt is being expanded into a reusable Apeironix Skill with structured inputs, insurance-specific workflow logic, decision rules, output requirements, quality checks, guardrails, and human-review points.

The goal is to move from:

**“Here is a useful prompt.”**

to:

**“Here is a repeatable insurance capability.”**

---

## From Prompts to Skills

A prompt tells AI what to do.

A Skill provides a repeatable framework for how the work should be performed.

Each Apeironix Skill is designed to include:

- A clearly defined purpose
- Appropriate use cases
- Required and optional inputs
- Source hierarchy
- Step-by-step workflow logic
- Insurance-specific decision rules
- Structured output requirements
- Quality-control checks
- Guardrails
- Human-review requirements
- Example use
- Version history

This structure is intended to make the original prompts more consistent, reusable, auditable, and adaptable across AI assistants, agents, and insurance workflows.

## Why We Are Publishing These Skills

Insurance professionals spend significant time performing work that is necessary but highly repeatable.

Examples include:

- Reviewing policies
- Preparing renewals
- Comparing coverage
- Summarizing account information
- Preparing proposals
- Analyzing submissions
- Drafting client communications
- Organizing underwriting information
- Reviewing claims and loss information
- Preparing meeting materials
- Identifying follow-up actions

AI can help with much of this work.

But useful insurance automation requires more than a generic prompt.

It requires context, workflow discipline, insurance-specific logic, quality controls, and human accountability.

That is what the Apeironix Skills framework is designed to provide.

## Quality and Safety Principles

Every Apeironix Skill should follow several core principles.

## Accuracy Over Completion

Do not invent insurance information simply to complete a task.

## Materiality Over Volume

Prioritize information that could materially affect coverage, cost, risk, compliance, client decisions, or agency operations.

## No Silent Assumptions

If an assumption is required, disclose it.

## Facts vs. Recommendations

Clearly distinguish documented information from observations and professional recommendations.

## Human Review

AI supports the insurance professional.

It does not replace licensed judgment, carrier authority, legal advice, compliance review, client authorization, or binding authority.
